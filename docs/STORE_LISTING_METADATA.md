## 4. Google Play Families Policy & COPPA Compliance

1. **Target Age**: Selected 6–8 and 9–12 years old.
2. **Ads Policy**: Zero in-app advertising (No AdMob, No Unity Ads, No Facebook Audience Network).
3. **Neutral Age Screen / Parental Gate**:
   - Adult Parental Gate (`ParentalGateModal.tsx`) placed on:
     - External links (`Linking.openURL`).
     - Account deletion requests.
     - Family Code generation and linking.
4. **Child Safety Compliance**:
   - Children accounts have no email address or real name requirements.
   - Sentry telemetry automatically scrubs IP addresses and masks child identifiers (`lib/observability.ts`).
   - Photos uploaded as proof are private and inaccessible to unauthorized users (enforced by Firebase Storage rules).
5. **Compliant SDKs**:
   - Only Google Firebase Core/Auth/Firestore and Expo official modules used. No non-certified behavioral analytics SDKs installed.
